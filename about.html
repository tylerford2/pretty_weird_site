Our Story

That's Pretty Weird.

Founded in 2026, Pretty Weird Apparel Co. makes small-batch and custom apparel for people who like their style a little off-center.

Why We Started

Not every shirt needs to make sense

Pretty Weird started with a simple idea: apparel doesn't have to be safe to be good. We design pieces that are a little bold, a little strange, and made in small enough batches that you won't see yourself coming and going.

Every design is printed using DTF (direct-to-film) transfer — a process that lets us get vivid, detailed color on demand without holding a warehouse of inventory or printing more than what's actually ordered.

✚

Small Batch By Design

We print in limited runs on purpose. When it's gone, it's gone — until the next drop.

How It's Made

What is DTF printing?

Direct-to-film (DTF) printing transfers a full-color design onto a special film, then heat-presses it onto the garment. It's how we get sharp detail, bold gradients, and prints that hold up wash after wash — on cotton, blends, and dark or light fabric alike.
